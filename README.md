# 1. AI-Based Personalized Drug Treatment

**Book Chapter (Chapter 12)**

**Book:** *Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Drug Design and Development*

**Publisher:** Wiley

**Publication Date:** June 2024

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1002/9781394234196.ch12

### Authors

- M. Shanmuga Sundari
- Harshini Reddy Penthala
- **Akshita Mogullapalli**
- Mayukha Mandya Ammangatambu

### About

This chapter explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming personalized drug treatment by leveraging patient-specific data such as genetics, clinical history, and lifestyle factors to improve treatment effectiveness.

The chapter discusses:

- Precision Medicine
- AI-assisted Drug Discovery
- Machine Learning for Treatment Recommendation
- Genomic Data Analysis
- Healthcare Decision Support Systems
- Challenges and Future Directions of AI in Personalized Medicine

# 2. Women and Millets: Historical Perspective

**Book Chapter (Chapter 11)**

**Book:** *The Role of Women in Cultivating Sustainable Societies Through Millets*

**Publisher:** IGI Global

**Publication Year:** 2023

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.4018/978-1-6684-9819-4.ch011

### Authors

- Shanmuga Sundari
- Harshini Reddy Penthala
- **Akshita Mogullapalli**
- Vidyullatha Sukhayasi
- Anand Nayyar

### About

This chapter presents a historical study of women's contributions to millet cultivation and their role in building sustainable societies.

The chapter discusses:

- Historical importance of millets
- Women's role in agriculture
- Nutritional significance of millets
- Food security
- Sustainable farming practices
- Socio-economic impact of millet cultivation
